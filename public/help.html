📦 Orders

How do I track my order?
Go to the Track Order page and enter your order ID. You'll see real-time status updates from order placement to delivery.
Where can I see my order history?
Login to your account and visit the Dashboard to view all your past orders, current orders, and order details.
Can I cancel my order?
Orders can be cancelled before they are shipped. Please contact customer support immediately with your order ID to request cancellation.
How long does delivery take?
Standard delivery takes 5-7 business days from order confirmation. You'll receive an estimated delivery date when you place your order.

💳 Payments

What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), debit cards, and digital payment methods. All transactions are secured with SSL encryption.
Is my payment information secure?
Yes! We use industry-standard encryption and never store your complete credit card information. All payments are processed through secure payment gateways.
When will I be charged?
Your payment method is charged immediately when you place an order. You'll receive an email confirmation with payment details.

🚚 Shipping

Do you offer free shipping?
Yes! We offer free standard shipping on all orders. No minimum purchase required.
How do I track my shipment?
Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number via email. You can also track your order anytime on our Track Order page using your order ID.
What if my package is lost or damaged?
If your package is lost or arrives damaged, please contact customer support immediately. We'll investigate and send a replacement or issue a refund.

↩️ Returns & Refunds

What is your return policy?
We accept returns within 30 days of delivery for most items. Products must be unused, in original packaging, and in resellable condition.
How do I return an item?
Contact customer support with your order ID to initiate a return. We'll provide return instructions and a prepaid shipping label if applicable.
When will I receive my refund?
Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive your returned item. The refund will be credited to your original payment method.
Can I exchange an item?
Yes! If you'd like to exchange an item for a different size or color, contact customer support. We'll arrange the exchange for you.

👤 Account

How do I create an account?
Click on Register in the navigation menu. Fill in your name, email, and password to create your account. It only takes a minute!
I forgot my password. What should I do?
Click the "Forgot Password" link on the Login page. Enter your email address and we'll send you instructions to reset your password.
How do I update my account information?
Login to your account and visit your Dashboard. You can update your profile information, email, and password from there.
Can I delete my account?
Yes. Please contact customer support to request account deletion. Note that this action is permanent and cannot be undone.

🛒 Shopping

How do I search for products?
Use the search bar at the top of any page. You can filter by category, price range, and availability. Sort results by price or rating to find exactly what you need.
What is a wishlist?
A wishlist lets you save products you're interested in for later. Click the heart icon on any product to add it to your wishlist.
How do I compare products?
Click the "Compare" button on product pages to add them to comparison. Visit the Comparison page to see side-by-side details of up to 3 products.
Can I modify my cart after checkout?
Once you've completed checkout, the order cannot be modified. However, you can cancel the order before it ships and place a new order with the correct items.
What is the Smart Product Assistant?
The Smart Assistant is a chat feature that helps answer questions about products. Ask about features, stock availability, comparisons, and purchase recommendations. Click the "Ask Assistant" button on any product page.
